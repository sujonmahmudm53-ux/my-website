How to Stay Safe While Using Public Wi-Fi

Public Wi-Fi can be convenient when you are in a cafe, hotel, airport, library, or other public place. However, using an unfamiliar network can create security risks. A few simple habits can help protect your information.

Use Trusted Wi-Fi Networks

Before connecting, check the official Wi-Fi name provided by the business or organization. Avoid connecting to networks with suspicious or unfamiliar names.

Avoid Sensitive Activities

When using public Wi-Fi, avoid entering sensitive information unless you are confident that the connection and website are secure. If possible, wait until you are using a trusted network for important activities.

Use HTTPS Websites

Look for HTTPS in the website address when browsing. HTTPS helps protect data exchanged between your device and the website.

Turn Off Automatic Wi-Fi Connection

Check your phone or computer settings and consider disabling automatic connection to unknown Wi-Fi networks. This gives you more control over which networks your device joins.

Keep Your Device Updated

Regular software and security updates can help protect your device against known security problems. Keep your operating system and important applications updated whenever possible.

Forget Public Networks After Use

If you no longer need a public Wi-Fi network, you can remove or forget it from your device. This can prevent your device from automatically trying to reconnect to that network later.

Use Mobile Data When Appropriate

If you are unsure about a public Wi-Fi network, using your mobile data may be a better option for sensitive activities.

Conclusion

Public Wi-Fi is useful, but it is important to use it carefully. Connect only to trusted networks, avoid sensitive activities when possible, keep your device updated, and control automatic Wi-Fi connections. These simple habits can help you use public networks more safely.
